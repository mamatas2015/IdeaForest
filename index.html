Where do customers get let down, and what is the smallest fix that would change that?

You have seen the customer journey up close, from the airport floor or from the passenger seat. This workshop is about the moments where a promise made to the customer is not delivered the same way every time, on the ground or onboard, and the practical fixes we could actually make.

Three kinds of fix we are looking for

GovernanceData, dashboards, reports and design standards. Example: Disruption dashboard.
ProcessA policy, SOP or new way of working. Example: changes to excess baggage waiver policy which is more easily governed.
AutomationA system does it instantly. Example: eVouchers as alternative payment mode or a single centralised compensation eligibility check.

What “doable” means here

The way forward would come from feasible ideas.

No big capital spendNo new aircraft, airport infrastructure or large equipment purchase.
First version in about 6 monthsA small first step counts. Start small, then grow.
Fits the three fixesGovernance, process or automation, working with our partner teams.
